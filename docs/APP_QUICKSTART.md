# App quick start

1. Open `/frcScoutingAppV2/`. Scouts enter the name assigned under **Assign Scouters**. The administrator signs in as `admin6560` with the team admin password.
2. As admin, use **Select Matches** to choose the event and load its schedule.
3. Use **Assign Scouters** to give each scout an alliance and driver-station position.
4. On the admin menu, paste the YouTube livestream into **Livestream Worker** and press **Start worker**. It waits for the stream and starts collecting when the match clock begins ticking.
5. Scouts open their assigned match, track the robot’s actions during the match, and submit when finished.
6. Use **CV Tracker** for capture details and recording/VOD analysis. Use **Scout Workspace** for rankings, team analysis, strategy, and picklists.
7. At the end of the event, confirm every match is present and review any **flagged** or **unidentified** captures. Press **Stop worker** when monitoring is no longer needed.
